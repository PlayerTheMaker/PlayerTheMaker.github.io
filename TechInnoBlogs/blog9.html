3/16 - 3/20

This week marks the beginning of the construction of what will eventually turn into our final product. We'll replace some of the materials towards the end to make it look nice, but functionally what we're making is the final product.

I got a piece of wood from engineering and screwed a bunch of joysticks into it. This will definetly be sturdier than the tape on styrofoam.

We ordered and Arduino UNO Mega so we aren't practically limited in the number of MUX boards we can connect to it. I used some of these straight breadboard wires to make sure it doesn't turn into too much of a wire jungle. Weston soldered some more MUX boards, one of which is seen here. (He also programmed sustain pedal functionally, but we don't actually have a pedal yet...)

Here's the current setup. We'll probably replace the styrofoam with wood and replace our current wood plank with a nicer one. In fact, it might be a good idea to lasercut the holes in the wood for additional accuracy.
Yaaawn, at of the time of writing this I'm tired. I'm excited though! I can see the project beginning to coalesce into something halfway decent.

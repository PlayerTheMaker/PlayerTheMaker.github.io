4/13 - 4/17

I mainly worked on the physical components of our project this week (while tackling some bugs which may not be entirely fixed [hence the lack of commits this week]).

I finished up that 3D model from last week and got to laser-cutting the parts of our instrument case in engineering.


Then, at the end of the week, I glued some square-stock to the base of the box so that I can mount the sides of it on next week (as you can see I used the amp as a weight).
Code wise, I mainly worked on fixing some bugs to do with using the keys as buttons to change synth instruments and settings. Part of the issue is electrical/physical, in that the voltage values aren't consistent due to all the keys sharing a power source and affecting the "pushed-in" voltage reading of other keys when pushed in. There isn't an easy way to fix this without scrapping the use of the keys as buttons entirely, so I've decided to just account for it in the code.

It seemed to be working pretty well by the end of the week, but I didn't commit it because I wasn't 100% sure (and I was busy working on the physical side of the project). I really should have, though.
This week Sebastian Lague made the source for his instrument sythesis coding adventure video freely available. Weston opened the project and started tinkering with it to hopefully get some more instrument presets out of it in the form of overtones generated from wave files with Fourier Transforms.
(If the frame doesn't load, here's the link to the video in question: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rRnOtKlg4jA)
Hopefully we'll get the instrument assembled next week, and then we just have to polish some featurea and wrap things up!
